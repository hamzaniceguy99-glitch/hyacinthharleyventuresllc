What we rent, in detail

Four categories for weddings, parties and corporate events, delivered and set up.

Program 01 · Anchored to the ground you have

Tents & canopies

Anchoring depends entirely on what is underneath — grass, sand, asphalt or a slab all need different methods, which is why we look before quoting. Larger tents may need a permit and fire marshal inspection, and we arrange that rather than discovering it on the day.

  • Any venue
  • Quoted after site check

Program 02 · Counted, then counted again

Tables, chairs & seating

Round or banquet changes how many people fit and how the room flows, and most hosts order by instinct. We work from the actual guest count and a layout, and we deliver a small margin over it because somebody always brings a plus one.
